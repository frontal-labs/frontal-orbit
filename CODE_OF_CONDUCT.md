# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful and inclusive**: Treat all individuals with respect and consideration
- **Use welcoming language**: Be mindful of your language and avoid exclusionary terms
- **Focus on what is best for the community**: Put the community's interests first
- **Show empathy**: Consider different perspectives and experiences
- **Be collaborative**: Work together constructively and help others succeed
- **Gracefully accept constructive criticism**: Be open to feedback and improvement
- **Ask for help when needed**: It's okay to not know everything
- **Step in and help**: Support others who may be experiencing harassment

### Unacceptable Behavior

- **Harassment**: Any conduct that creates a hostile environment
- **Discriminatory language**: Comments or actions that marginalize or exclude
- **Personal attacks**: Insults, demeaning comments, or personal criticism
- **Unwelcome sexual attention**: Any form of sexual harassment
- **Trolling**: Deliberate disruption of discussions
- **Spam**: Unsolicited promotional content
- **Doxxing**: Sharing private information without consent
- **Threats**: Any form of threatening behavior
- **Retaliation**: Punishing someone for reporting conduct

## Application to All Spaces

This code of conduct applies to all community spaces, including but not limited to:

- **GitHub repositories**: Issues, pull requests, discussions
- **Communication channels**: Slack, email lists
- **Events**: Conferences, meetups, workshops
- **Private communications**: Direct messages and emails
- **Social media**: Interactions on platforms like Twitter/X

### Online Community Guidelines

#### GitHub Interactions
- **Issue reporting**: Provide clear, constructive bug reports and feature requests
- **Pull requests**: Welcome contributions and provide helpful review
- **Code reviews**: Focus on technical merits, be respectful and constructive
- **Discussions**: Keep conversations on-topic and professional

#### Slack Guidelines
- **Be helpful**: Assist others who ask questions
- **Stay on topic**: Use appropriate channels for different discussions
- **No spam**: Avoid repetitive messages or off-topic content
- **Respect privacy**: Don't share private conversations or information

#### Event Conduct
- **Respect speakers**: Listen attentively and ask questions respectfully
- **Network professionally**: Make connections in a respectful manner
- **Accommodate needs**: Be mindful of accessibility requirements
- **Report issues**: Notify organizers of any conduct concerns

## Enforcement

### Reporting Process

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to:

- **Email**: conduct@frontal.dev
- **GitHub**: Report to any maintainer or use GitHub's reporting tools
- **Events**: Speak with event organizers or staff

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially.

### Response Guidelines

Our community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

#### 1. Correction
- **Impact**: Minor violation that can be resolved with education
- **Action**: Private warning, clarification of expectations
- **Goal**: Help the contributor understand and correct the issue

#### 2. Warning
- **Impact**: Repeated minor violations or single moderate violation
- **Action**: Public or private warning, temporary restrictions
- **Goal**: Encourage reflection and behavior change

#### 3. Temporary Ban
- **Impact**: Serious violation or pattern of inappropriate behavior
- **Action**: Temporary removal from community spaces
- **Duration**: Typically 1-4 weeks, depending on severity
- **Goal**: Protect the community while allowing for redemption

#### 4. Permanent Ban
- **Impact**: Severe violations, threats, or repeated harmful behavior
- **Action**: Permanent removal from all community spaces
- **Scope**: All platforms and community interactions
- **Goal**: Ensure community safety and well-being

### Enforcement Process

1. **Report received**: Community leaders review the report
2. **Investigation**: Gather information from all parties involved
3. **Decision**: Determine appropriate response based on guidelines
4. **Action**: Implement consequences and communicate to involved parties
5. **Follow-up**: Monitor situation and ensure resolution

### Appeals Process

If you believe a consequence was unjust, you may appeal by:

1. **Written appeal**: Email conduct@frontal.dev with detailed explanation
2. **Review**: Different community leaders will review the appeal
3. **Decision**: Final determination within 7 business days
4. **Communication**: Outcome communicated to all parties

## Community Guidelines

### Contributing Guidelines

#### Before Contributing
- **Read documentation**: Familiarize yourself with project guidelines
- **Check existing issues**: Avoid duplicating existing work or reports
- **Ask questions**: Use appropriate channels for clarification
- **Start small**: Begin with documentation or minor improvements

#### During Contribution
- **Be patient**: Reviews take time, be respectful of reviewers' schedules
- **Accept feedback**: Consider all constructive criticism
- **Communicate clearly**: Explain your changes and reasoning
- **Follow standards**: Adhere to project coding and documentation standards

#### After Contribution
- **Respond to reviews**: Address feedback in a timely manner
- **Celebrate success**: Acknowledge merged contributions
- **Help others**: Share knowledge and assist new contributors
- **Stay engaged**: Continue participating in community discussions

### Communication Guidelines

#### Constructive Feedback
- **Be specific**: Provide clear, actionable suggestions
- **Focus on code**: Address technical aspects, not personal attributes
- **Explain reasoning**: Help others understand your perspective
- **Offer solutions**: Suggest improvements rather than just identifying problems

#### Conflict Resolution
- **Assume good faith**: Believe others have positive intentions
- **Seek understanding**: Ask questions to clarify perspectives
- **Find common ground**: Focus on shared goals and values
- **Escalate if needed**: Involve moderators for unresolved conflicts

## Diversity and Inclusion

### Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a diverse and inclusive community where:

- **All voices are heard**: Everyone has opportunity to contribute
- **Different perspectives are valued**: Diversity strengthens our community
- **Barriers are removed**: We actively work to eliminate obstacles
- **Learning is continuous**: We educate ourselves on inclusion issues

### Inclusive Language Guidelines

- **Use gender-neutral language**: Avoid assumptions about gender
- **Respect pronouns**: Use individuals' preferred pronouns
- **Avoid cultural references**: Be mindful of cultural differences
- **Consider accessibility**: Ensure content is accessible to all

### Accessibility

We strive to make our community accessible to everyone:

- **Documentation**: Provide clear, well-structured documentation
- **Communication**: Use clear, simple language in discussions
- **Events**: Ensure physical and digital accessibility
- **Tools**: Choose accessible platforms and tools
